The network

Built from a
collective, not a roster.

Most studios sign an artist and build a sound around them. Athalias runs the other direction: artists connected through the MusicCollabNetwork and SkateTribune communities bring their own voice in, and the track gets built at the point where those voices meet.

Hip-hop is the loudest thread, but it isn't the only one. The catalog runs from experimental rap and cloud-leaning production into indie dream pop, and the sound keeps shifting because the people and projects keep changing: a MusicCollabNetwork session one week, a personal dream-pop record the next.

Deadboi.Walking produces most of the beats himself in Logic and FL Studio, plays piano and guitar on top of them, and also builds custom instrumentals for outside brands. Alongside his own catalog, he handles placements, distribution and marketing for hundreds of other rappers and producers across the network, all free of charge.

Deadboi.Walking also runs SkateTribune, where all of it (Athalias, ClitLitter, MusicCollabNetwork) actually gets used: skateboarders, fingerboarders and gamers cut it against their own footage, and the community cuts skate clips back into the music. It's one connected loop, not two separate worlds, and the monthly edit challenge below runs on exactly that loop.

Monthly edit challenge

Hosted with SkateTribune and Dan Gogh. Open entry, no following required.

Featuring Danny Tate

Danny Tate is a Grammy-nominated songwriter who's written for Rick Springfield, Lynyrd Skynyrd and Tim McGraw. His "Affair of the Heart" was nominated against Michael Jackson's "Beat It." Each month, one of his tracks becomes the soundtrack for an open call.

Cut something to it: skate footage, Skate 4, Skater XL, dance, art, film, whatever you've got. Sixty seconds max. Danny reviews every entry himself and picks one winner a month for a free prize. No big following needed, just something worth watching.
